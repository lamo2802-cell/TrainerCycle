Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy explains what information TrainerCycle collects, why, and what you can do about it. TrainerCycle is operated by Stars and Stiles Ltd, trading as TrainerCycle ("we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales (company number: 14294081, registered office: 19 Ermine Street, Broughton, Brigg, DN20 0AH, England).

What we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile photo if you sign in with Google; or your email address and a securely hashed password if you create an account directly (we never store or see your actual password — it's hashed using industry-standard methods before it ever reaches our database).
  • Training data — your FTP (Functional Threshold Power), workouts you build or save, and training plans you follow.
  • Ride and session data — power, heart rate, cadence, and timing data recorded during a live session via Bluetooth-connected devices (a trainer, heart rate strap), and completed strength sessions.
  • Subscription and payment status — whether your subscription is active, via Stripe. We do not see or store your card details — Stripe handles payment directly and securely.
  • Third-party connections — if you choose to connect Strava, we store the access tokens needed to upload rides on your behalf.
  • Marketing preference — whether you've opted in to receive occasional emails from us, and when you gave or withdrew that choice. You can change this any time in Settings.
Power, heart rate, and cadence data can be considered health-related information. We only use it to power the app's own features (showing your charts, calculating training metrics, syncing rides) — never sold, and never used for advertising.

Why we collect it

  • To provide the core service — storing your workouts, tracking your FTP, running live sessions, and keeping your ride history.
  • To process your subscription payment and keep your access up to date.
  • To upload rides to Strava, only if and when you explicitly connect that account.
  • To send you occasional training tips and product updates by email — only if you've explicitly opted in, and never otherwise.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores your account and training data securely.
  • Google — used only if you sign in with Google; we receive your name, email, and photo, nothing else.
  • Resend — our email delivery provider, used to send account emails (password resets, confirmations) and, only with your consent, marketing emails.
  • Stripe — processes payments directly; we never handle your card details ourselves.
  • Strava — only if you connect your account, solely to upload the rides you choose to export.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for advertising purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data yourself at any time from Settings — cancel any active subscription first, then use "Delete My Account". Alternatively, contact us using the details below and we'll action it promptly.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to how it's processed — including withdrawing marketing consent at any time, either in Settings or via the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. To exercise any of these, get in touch using the contact details below.

Cookies

We use only the minimum technical cookies/local storage needed to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

TrainerCycle is not directed at, or intended for use by, children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your data, can be sent to privacy@trainercycle.com.
